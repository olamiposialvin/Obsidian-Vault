1. The points $D(2, 1)$, $E(1, 5)$, and $F(-6, -1)$ are the mid-points of the sides $AC$, $AB$, and $BC$ respectively of $\Delta ABC$.
	1. Show that $\Delta DEF$ is right angled.
	2. Find the coordinate of $B$.
	3. Find the equation of $AC$.
2.  Two straight lines are $3x + 2y - 5 = 0$ and $x - 2y - 7 = 0$. Find the equation of a line passing through their point of intersection and parallel to the line $x + 2y - 5 = 0$.[^1]
3. Find the value of $\cos (-300^\circ)$ in surd form.[^2]

# Solution
## Question 1

$$
\HUGE\textbf{Data}
\\[1em]
\begin{aligned}
A &= (x_1, y_1)
\\
B &= (x_2, y_2)
\\
C &= (x_3, y_3)
\\
D &= (2, 1)
\\
E &= (1, 5)
\\
F &= (-6, -1)
\end{aligned}
$$
$$
\LARGE\text{Equations}
\\[1em]
\begin{array}{c}
D_x = \frac{x_1 + x_3}{2} & D_y = \frac{y_1 + y_3}{2}
\\
E_x = \frac{x_1 + x_2}{2} & E_y = \frac{y_1 + y_2}{2}
\\
F_x = \frac{x_2 + x_3}{2} & F_y = \frac{y_2 + y_3}{2}

\end{array}
$$

## Question 2 (2a)

$$
\Huge\textbf{Data}
\\[1em]
\begin{aligned}
L &: y = mx + c
\\
L_1 &: 3x + 2y - 5 = 0
\\
L_2 &: x - 2y - 7 = 0
\\
L_3 &: x + 2y - 5 = 0
\end{aligned}
\\[2em]
\LARGE\textbf{Later Obtained}
\\[1em]
\begin{aligned}
x &= 3
\\
y &= -2
\\
m &= -\frac{1}{2}
\\
c &= -\frac{1}{2}
\end{aligned}
$$
$$
\Huge\textbf{Relations}
\\[1em]
   \text{$L_1$ and $L_2$ intersect at point $(x, y)$}
\\ \text{$L$ and $L_3$ are parallel $(m = m_3)$}
$$
$$
\LARGE\text{Point of Intersection}
\\[0.75em]
\begin{array}{r@{\quad \hcline{3em} \;}l}
3x + 2y - 5 = 0 & \left< i \right>
\\
x - 2y - 7 = 0 & \left< ii \right>
\end{array}
\\[1em]
\text{Add $\left< i \right>$ and $\left< ii \right>$ together}
\\[1em]
3x + 2y - 5 + (x - 2y - 7) = 0 + 0
\\
3x \cancel{+ 2y} - 5 + x \cancel{- 2y} - 7 = 0
\\
4x - 12 = 0
\\
4x = 12
\\[0.5em]
\boxed{x = 3}
\\[2em]
\text{In $\left< i \right>$}
\\[1em]
3(3) + 2y - 5 = 0
\\
9 + 2y - 5 = 0
\\
4 + 2y = 0
\\
2y = -4
\\[0.5em]
\boxed{y = -2}
\\[2.5em]
\therefore \text{The point of intersection is $\boxed{(3, -2)}$}
$$
$$
\LARGE\text{Gradient (Parallelism)}
\\[0.75em]
L_3: x + 2y - 5 = 0
\\
2y = -x + 5
\\
y = \enclose{}{-\frac{1}{2}} \, x + \frac{5}{2}
\\[0.75em]
\boxed{m_3 = -\frac{1}{2}}
\\[2em]
\text{Since lines $L$ and $L_3$ are parallel, $m = m_3$}
\\[0.5em]
\boxed{m = -\frac{1}{2}}
$$
$$
\LARGE\text{Y$\!-$Intercept}
\\[0.75em]
L : y = mx + c
\\
(-2) = \left( -\tfrac{1}{2} \right)(3) + c
\\
-2 = -\tfrac{3}{2} + c
\\
\tfrac{3}{2} -2 = c
\\
\tfrac{3}{2} - \tfrac{4}{2} = c
\\
{-\tfrac{1}{2}} = c
\\[0.75em]
\boxed{c = -\tfrac{1}{2}}
$$
$$
\Huge\textbf{Equation}
\\[0.75em]
\Large\boxed{y = -\frac{1}{2}x - \frac{1}{2}}
$$

## Question 3 (2b)

$$
\cos (-300^\circ) = \cos (300^\circ)
\\
\footnotesize{(\cos \text{is an even function})}
\\[0.75em]
\cos (300^\circ) = + \cos (360 - 300)
\\
\footnotesize{(\text{300$^\circ$ lies on the 4th quadrant, where $\cos$ is positive})}
\\[0.75em]
\cos (300^\circ) = + \cos (60)
\\[1em]
\boxed{\cos (300^\circ) = + \frac{1}{2}}
$$
