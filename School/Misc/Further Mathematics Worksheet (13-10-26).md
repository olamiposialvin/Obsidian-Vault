1. Find the distance between the points $X(3p, 6q)$ and $Y(6p, 3q)$.

	$$D = \sqrt{  \left( x_2 - x_1 \right) ^2 +  \left( y_2 - y_1 \right) ^2}$$
	$$D = \sqrt{  \left( 6p - 3p \right) ^2 +  \left( 3q - 6q \right) ^2}$$
	$$D = \sqrt{  \left( 3p \right) ^2 +  \left( -3q \right) ^2}$$
	$$D = \sqrt{ 9p^2 + 9q^2 }$$
	$$D = \sqrt{ 9 \left( p^2 + q^2 \right)  }$$
	$$D = 3\sqrt{ p^2 + q^2 }$$

> [!note] Answer
> The distance between points $X$ and $Y$ is $3\sqrt{ p^2 + q^2 }$.

3. Find the acute angle between the lines $4x + 6y = 2$ and $16x - 28y = 20$. 

	**Equation 1:** $4x + 6y = 2$
		$$6y = 2 - 4x$$
		$$y = \frac{2}{6} - \frac{4}{6}x$$
		$$y = \frac{1}{3} - \frac{2}{3}x$$
		$$m_1 = - \frac{2}{3}$$
	
	**Equation 2:** $16x - 28y = 20$
		$$16x - 20 = 28y$$
		$$y =\frac{16}{28}x - \frac{20}{28}$$
		$$y =\frac{4}{7}x - \frac{5}{7}$$
		$$m_2 = \frac{4}{7}$$

	$$\tan \left( α \right)  = \left| \frac{m_2 - m_1}{1 + m_2 m_1} \right|$$
	$$\tan \left( α \right)  = \left| \frac{\frac{4}{7} -  \left(  - \frac{2}{3}  \right) }{1 + \frac{4}{7} \cdot - \frac{2}{3}} \right|$$
	$$\tan \left( α \right)  = \left| \frac{26}{21} \div \frac{13}{21} \right|$$
	$$\tan \left( α \right)  = \left| \frac{26}{21} \cdot \frac{21}{13} \right|$$
	$$\tan \left( α \right)  = \left| \frac{26}{13} \right|$$
	$$\tan \left( α \right)  = 2$$
	$$α = \tan^{-1} \left(  2  \right) $$
	$$α = 63.4349 ^\circ$$

> [!note] Answer
> The acute angle between the lines is $63.4349 ^\circ$

4. The line $L_1$ has equation $y = 6x - 9$. Another line $L_2$ has gradient $m$. The acute angle between the lines $L_1$ and $L_2$ is $30 ^\circ$. Find the two possible values for $m$.

	**Equation 1:** $y = 6x - 9$
	$$m_1 = 6$$
	
	**Equation 2:** $m_2 = m$
	
	$$\tan \left( α \right)  = \left| \frac{m_2 - m_1}{1 + m_2 m_1} \right|$$
	$$\tan \left( 30 ^\circ \right)  = \left| \frac{m - 6}{1 + 6m} \right|$$
	$$\tan \left( 30 ^\circ \right)  = \left| \frac{m - 6}{1 + 6m} \right|$$
	$$\frac{\sqrt{3}}{3} = \left| \frac{m - 6}{1 + 6m} \right|$$
	
	**Case 1:** $\frac{\sqrt{3}}{3} = \frac{m - 6}{1 + 6m}$
	$$\sqrt{3}  \left( 1 + 6m \right)  = 3 \left( m - 6 \right)$$
	$$\sqrt{3} + 6 \sqrt{3} m = 3m - 18$$
	$$\sqrt{3} + 18 = 3m - 6 \sqrt{3} m$$
	$$\sqrt{3} + 18 = m  \left(  3 - 6 \sqrt{3}  \right)$$
	$$m = \frac{\sqrt{3} + 18}{3 - 6 \sqrt{3}} \approx -2.6693$$
	
	**Case 2:** $-\frac{\sqrt{3}}{3} = \frac{m - 6}{1 + 6m}$
	$$-\sqrt{3}  \left( 1 + 6m \right) = 3 \left( m - 6 \right)$$
	$$-\sqrt{3} - 6 \sqrt{3} m = 3m - 18$$
	$$18 -\sqrt{3} = 3m + 6 \sqrt{3} m$$
	$$18 -\sqrt{3} = m \left( 3 + 6 \sqrt{3} \right)$$
	$$m = \frac{18 -\sqrt{3}}{3 + 6 \sqrt{3}} \approx 1.2147$$

> [!note] Answer
> The two possible values for $m$ are $-2.6693$ and $1.2147$.

5. A straight line $2x + 5y + 12 = 0$ passes through the point $(k,-2)$ and is perpendicular to $5x - 2y + 3 = 0$. Find the value of $k$.

	Take $x$ as $k$ and $y$ as $-2$.
	$$2k + 5\left( -2 \right) + 12 = 0$$
	$$2k - 10 + 12 = 0$$
	$$2k + 2 = 0$$
	$$2k = -2$$
	$$k = -1$$

> [!note] Answer
> The value of $k$ is $-1$.


6. Find the angle of the slope of a line if the gradient is $\frac{9}{10}$.

9. A straight line has gradient $\sqrt{3}$. Find its angle of inclination.

	$$α = \tan^{-1}\left( \sqrt{3} \right)$$
	$$α = 60 ^\circ$$

10. A line has gradient $-1$. Find its angle of inclination, measured anticlockwise from the positive x-axis.

	$$m = \tan\left( α \right)$$
	$$-1 = \tan\left( α \right)$$
	$$α = \tan^{-1}\left( -1 \right)$$
	$$\tan^{-1}\left( -1 \right) = -45 ^\circ$$
	Since $\tan^{-1}$ returns a negative result, $α$ is obtuse:
	$$α = 180 ^\circ - 45 ^\circ$$
	$$α = 135 ^\circ$$

> [!note] Answer
> The angle of inclination, measured anticlockwise from the positive x-axis for the line is $135 ^\circ$
