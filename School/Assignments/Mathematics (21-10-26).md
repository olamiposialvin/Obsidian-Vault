The length and width of a rectangle are 3.2 cm by 2.6 cm to 1 d.p.

1. Write down the smallest and largest possible length of the rectangle.
	$$
	\text{Approximated Unit A.U. = 1 d.p. = 0.1}
	$$
	$$
	\text{Limit of Accuracy L.A. = } \pm \frac{\text{A.U.}}{2}
	$$
	$$
	\text{L.A.} = \pm \frac{0.1}{2}
	$$
	$$
	\text{L.A.} = \pm 0.05 
	$$
	$$
	\text{Lower Bound L.B.} = 3.2 - 0.05
	$$
	$$
	\text{L.B.} = 3.15
	$$
	$$
	\text{Upper Bound U.B.} = 3.2 + 0.05
	$$
	$$
	\text{U.B.} = 3.25
	$$
	> [!note] Answer
	> The smallest and largest possible length of the rectangle are $3.15 \text{ cm}$ and $3.25 \text{ cm}$ respectively.
2. Write down the smallest and largest possible width of the rectangle.
	$$
	\text{Lower Bound L.B.} = 2.6 - 0.05
	$$
	$$
	\text{L.B.} = 2.55
	$$
	$$
	\text{Upper Bound U.B.} = 2.6 + 0.05
	$$
	$$
	\text{U.B.} = 2.65
	$$
	> [!note] Answer
	> The smallest and largest possible width of the rectangle are $2.55 \text{ cm}$ and $2.65 \text{ cm}$ respectively.
3. Calculate the largest possible perimeter and area the rectangle could have.
	$$
	\text{Upper Bound for length U.B.L.} = 2.65
	$$
	$$
	\text{Upper Bound for width U.B.W.} = 3.25
	$$
	$$
	\text{Upper Bound for Perimeter U.B.P.} = 2 \left( \text{U.B.L. + U.B.W} \right)
	$$
	$$
	\text{U.B.P.} = 2 \left( 2.65 + 3.25 \right)
	$$
	$$
	\text{U.B.P.} = 2 \left( 5.9 \right)
	$$
	$$
	\text{U.B.P.} = 11.8 \text{ cm}
	$$
	$$
	\text{Upper Bound for Area U.B.A.} = \text{U.B.L.} \times \text{U.B.W}
	$$
	$$
	\text{U.B.A.} = 2.65 \times 3.25
	$$
	$$
	\text{U.B.A.} = 8.6125 \text{ cm}^2
	$$
	> [!note] Answer
	> The largest possible perimeter and area the rectangle could have is $11.8 \text{ cm}$ and $8.6125 \text{ cm}^2$ respectively.
