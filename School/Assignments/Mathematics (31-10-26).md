1. Given that $\frac{5y - x}{8y + 3x} = \frac{1}{5}$, find the value of $\frac{x}{y}$ to two ($2$) decimal places.
	$$
	\text{Multiply both sides by $8y + 3x$ and $5$}
	$$
	$$
	\frac{(5y - x)(8y + 3x)(5)}{8y + 3x} = \frac{1(8y + 3x)(5)}{5}
	$$
	 $$
	(5y - x)(5) = 1(8y + 3x)
	$$
	$$
	25y - 5x = 8y + 3x
	$$
	$$
	\text{Add $5x$ and subtract $8y$ from both sides}
	$$
	$$
	25y - 5x + 5x - 8y = 8y + 3x + 5x - 8y
	$$
	$$
	17y = 8x
	$$
	$$
	\text{Divide both sides by $y$ and $8$}
	$$
	$$
	\frac{17y}{8y} = \frac{8x}{8y}
	$$
	$$
	\frac{x}{y} = \frac{17}{8}
	$$
	$$
	\frac{x}{y} = 2.125
	$$
	$$
	\frac{x}{y} \approx 2.13 \text{ to 2 d.p.}
	$$
	> [!note] Answer
	> The value of $\frac{x}{y}$ is $2.13$ to 2 d.p.
