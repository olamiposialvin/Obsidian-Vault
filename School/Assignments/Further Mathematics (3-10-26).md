The points $P(11, 7)$ divides a line segment joining $A(5, 3)$ and $B(8,5)$ externally at ratio $m : n$. Find $AP : PB$ ($m : n$)

# Solution 
$$P_x = \frac{mx_2 - nx_1}{m - n}$$
$$11 = \frac{8m - 5n}{m - n}$$
$$11(m - n) = 8m - 5n$$
$$11m - 11n = 8m - 5n$$
$$11m - 8m = 11n - 5n$$
$$3m = 6n$$
$$m = 2n$$
$$\frac{m}{n} = 2$$
$$\frac{m}{n} = \frac{2}{1}$$
$$∴ m : n = 2 : 1$$
$$AP : PB = 2 : 1$$

> [!note] Answer
> $AP : PB = 2 : 1$
