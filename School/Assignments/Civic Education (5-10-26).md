# Assigned

1. What is culture?
2. Mention 5 examples of Nigerian traditions or customs.
3. List 5 skills needed to preserve traditions, customs, and beliefs in Nigeria.
4. Explain 3 ways storytelling helps preserve Nigerian culture.
5. State 5 reasons why Nigerian traditions and customs should be preserved.

## Answer

### 1. What is culture?
Culture is the collective sum of beliefs, values, customs, behaviors, language, arts, and traditions that characterize a particular society or group of people. It represents a shared way of life that is passed down from generation to generation, shaping how individuals interact, view the world, and express their collective identity.

### 2. Mention 5 examples of Nigerian traditions or customs.
- **Traditional Greetings:** Specific respectful gestures are deeply ingrained, such as males prostrating and females kneeling when greeting elders in Yoruba culture.
- **Traditional Marriage Customs:** Practices like the payment of bride price and the presentation of specific items (e.g., kola nuts and palm wine) help preserve cultural identity by embodying rituals passed down through generations.
- **Traditional Festivals:** Events like the New Yam Festival in eastern Nigeria, the Osun-Oshogbo Festival in western Nigeria, and the Argungu Fishing Festival in northern Nigeria are celebrated periodically to honor heritage.
- **Traditional Dressing:** Wearing native attire during weddings, naming ceremonies, and chieftaincy titles serves as a source of pride and a visual representation of cultural heritage.
- **Traditional Food:** Native dishes are meals eaten by indigenous people and passed from generation to generation as a symbol of identity and connection to foundational beliefs.

### 3. List 5 skills needed to preserve traditions, customs, and beliefs in Nigeria.
- **Language Proficiency:** Citizens must learn to speak their indigenous languages fluently and proudly, as language plays an important role in communicating and preserving cultural beliefs.
- **Artifact Preservation:** The ability to protect and maintain objects of historical significance helps honor the culture and continue telling the ancestral story into the future.
- **Cultural Exhibition Organization:** The skill to plan and execute large public displays of art, cultural dances, and carnivals (like the Calabar Carnival) offers educational value to the growing generation.
- **Traditional Culinary and Artistic Skills:** Maintaining the knowledge of how to prepare traditional foods and create indigenous music ensures these vital parts of lifestyle and history remain alive.
- **Cultural Advocacy and Documentation:** The ability to research, document, and campaign for cultural awareness fosters national consciousness and protects traditions from being lost to time.

### 4. Explain 3 ways storytelling helps preserve Nigerian culture.
- **Transmission of Values and Norms:** Storytelling provides a solid basis for raising culturally adjusted individuals and inculcating core societal values or norms in the youth.
- **Preservation of Language and Heritage:** Sharing stories in native tongues preserves traditions, languages, and values, ensuring that Nigeria's cultural heritage continues to thrive across generations.
- **Passing Down History and Lessons:** Storytelling acts as a vital means of passing down history, values, lessons, and cultural norms that have been a cornerstone of Nigerian culture for centuries.

### 5. State 5 reasons why Nigerian traditions and customs should be preserved.
- **Maintains Identity and Pride:** Preserving this vast cultural heritage is essential for maintaining a strong sense of identity and national pride among citizens.
- **Promotes Unity:** Maintaining customs and traditions encourages unity and serves as a clear means of identification for what the nation is known for.
- **Educates Future Generations:** Keeping traditions alive ensures that younger generations are educated about their roots, history, and foundational beliefs.
- **Attracts Global Tourism:** Authentic cultural heritage, including festivals, traditional attire, and artifacts, is a major draw for global tourism, which can boost the local economy.
- **Builds Community and Understanding:** Celebrating diverse traditions, languages, and customs helps build pride, mutual understanding, and a stronger sense of community across different ethnic groups.
