2. $\frac{1}{6}y^2 = \frac{2}{3}y + \frac{16}{3}$
	$$
	\text{Multiply both sides by 6}
	$$
	$$
	\cancel{6} \left( \frac{1}{\cancel{6}}y^2 \right) = 6 \left( \frac{2}{3}y + \frac{16}{3} \right)
	$$
	$$
	y^2 = \frac{2 \times \cancelto{2}{6}}{\cancel{3}}y + \frac{16 \times \cancelto{2}{6}}{\cancel{3}} 
	$$
	$$
	y^2 = (2 \times 2)y + 16 \times 2
	$$
	$$
	y^2 = 4y + 32
	$$
	$$

	$$
	$$
