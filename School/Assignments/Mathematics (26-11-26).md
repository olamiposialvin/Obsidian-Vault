Find the quadratic equation whose roots are $-2$ and $7 \frac{1}{2} \quad (\frac{15}{2})$.

$$
\begin{array}{c@{\quad \text{or} \quad}c}
x = -2 & x = \tfrac{15}{2}
\\
x + 2 = 0 & x - \tfrac{15}{2} = 0
\end{array}
\\
(x + 2) \! \left( x - \tfrac{15}{2} \right) = 0
\\
(x + 2)(2x - 15) = 0
\\
x(2x - 15) + 2( 2x - 15 ) = 0
\\
2x^2 - 15x + 4x - 30 = 0
\\[1em]
\boxed{2x^2 - 11x - 30 = 0}
$$
