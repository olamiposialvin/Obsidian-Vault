## 1. Completing the Square

$$
8x^2 + 4x - 4 = 0
$$
$$
x^2 + \frac{4}{8}x - \frac{4}{8} = 0
$$
$$
x^2 + \frac{1}{2}x = \frac{1}{2}
$$
$$
x^2 + \frac{1}{2}x + \left(\frac{1}{4}\right)^2 = \left(\frac{1}{4}\right)^2 + \frac{1}{2}
$$
$$
\left(x + \frac{1}{4}\right)^2 = \frac{1}{16} + \frac{1}{2}
$$
$$
\left(x + \frac{1}{4}\right)^2 = \frac{1}{16} + \frac{8}{16}
$$
$$
\left(x + \frac{1}{4}\right)^2 = \frac{9}{16}
$$
$$
\left|x + \frac{1}{4}\right| = \sqrt{\frac{9}{16}}
$$
$$
x + \frac{1}{4} = \pm\frac{3}{4}
$$
$$
x = -\frac{1}{4} \pm \frac{3}{4}
$$
$$
\begin{array}{c@{\quad \text{or} \quad}c}
x = \frac{-1+3}{4} & x = \frac{-1-3}{4}
\\
x = \frac{2}{4} & x = \frac{-4}{4}
\\
x = \frac{\cancel{2}^{\,1}}{\cancel{4}_{\,2}} & x = -\frac{\cancel{4}}{\cancel{4}}
\\
\boxed{x = \frac{1}{2}} & \boxed{\text{or} \quad x = -1}
\end{array}
$$

## 2. Quadratic Formula

$$
8x^2 + 4x - 4 = 0
$$
$$
\text{Compare with $ax^2 + bx + c = 0$}
$$
$$
a = 8 \qquad b = 4 \qquad c = -4
$$
$$
x = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a}
$$
$$
x = \frac{-(4) \pm \sqrt{4^2 - 4(8)(-4)}}{2(8)}
$$
$$
x = \frac{-4 \pm \sqrt{16 + 128}}{16}
$$
$$
x = \frac{-4 \pm \sqrt{144}}{16}
$$
$$
x = \frac{-4 \pm 12}{16}
$$
$$
\begin{array}{c@{\quad \text{or} \quad}c}
x = \frac{-4+12}{16} & x = \frac{-4-12}{16}
\\
x = \frac{8}{16} & x = \frac{-16}{16}
\\
x = \frac{\cancel{8}^{\,1}}{\cancel{16}_{\,2}} & x = -\frac{\cancel{16}}{\cancel{16}}
\\
\boxed{x = \frac{1}{2}} & \boxed{x = -1}
\end{array}
$$
