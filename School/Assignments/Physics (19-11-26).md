1. An object in simple harmonic motion has an angular speed of $4.4 \, \text{rad} \, \text{s} ^-1$. Calculate the period of the motion.
$$
\omega = 4.4 \, \mathrm{rad \, s^{-1}}
$$
$$
T = \frac{2 \pi}{\omega}
$$
$$
T = \frac{2 \pi}{4.4}
$$
$$
\boxed{T = \frac{5}{11} \pi \, \mathrm{s}}
\\[1em]
\boxed{T \approx 1.43 \, \mathrm{s}}
$$
2. A simple pendulum of mass $50 \, \text{kg}$ oscillates with amplitude $4 \, \text{cm}$ and period $2.0 \, \text{s}$. Determine the angular and linear velocities of the bob when the velocity of the bob is maximum.
$$
\begin{aligned}
m &= 50 \, \mathrm{kg}
\\
A &= 4 \, \mathrm{cm} = 0.04 \, \mathrm{m}
\\
T &= 2.0 \, \mathrm{s}
\end{aligned}
$$
$$
\mathrm{v_{max}} = \omega A
$$
$$
\omega = \frac{2 \pi}{T}
$$
$$
\omega = \frac{2 \pi}{2}
$$
$$
\boxed{\omega = \pi \, \mathrm{rad \, s^{-1}}}
\\[1em]
\boxed{\omega \approx 3.14 \, \mathrm{rad \, s^{-1}}}
$$
$$
\mathrm{v_{max}} = \omega A
$$
$$
\mathrm{v_{max}} = \pi \times 0.04
$$
$$
\boxed{\mathrm{v_{max}} = \frac{1}{25}\pi \, \mathrm{m/s}}
\\[1em]
\boxed{\mathrm{v_{max}} = 0.13 \, \mathrm{m/s}}
$$
