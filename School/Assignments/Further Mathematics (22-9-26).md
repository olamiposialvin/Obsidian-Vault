A survey was recently carried out to find out the amount of job applications into three newspaper establishments

 - 70 people applied to Daily Times.
 - 65 people applied to Daily Graphic.
 - 85 people applied to Daily Punch. <br><br>




 - 40 people applied to Daily Times only.
 - 20 people applied to Daily Graphic only.
 - 45 people applied to Daily Punch only. <br><br>

 - 15 people applied to all three establishments.

How many people applied to:
1. Daily Times and Daily Graphic.
2. Daily Times and Daily Punch.
3. Daily Graphics and Daily Punch.
4. At least one establishment.
5. Two establishment only.
6. Exactly one establishment.

# Solution

## Variables and Formulae

Given the following:

- Daily Time = T = 70
- Daily Graphic = G = 65 
- Daily Punch = P = 85 <br><br>

- Daily Time only = To = 40
- Daily Graphic only = Go = 20
- Daily Punch only = Po = 45 <br><br>

- Daily Time and Graphic = TG
- Daily Time and Punch = TP
- Daily Graphic and Punch = GP <br><br>

- Daily Time and Graphic only = TGo
- Daily Time and Punch only = TPo
- Daily Graphic and Punch only = GPo <br><br>

- Daily Time, Graphic, and Punch (All three establishments) = TGP = 15

then:

- T = To + TGo + TPo + TGP
- G = Go + TGo + GPo + TGP
- P = Po + TPo + GPo + TGP <br><br>

- TG = TGo + TGP
- TP = TPo + TGP
- GP = GPo + TGP

## 1. Daily Times and Daily Graphic

TG = TGo + TGP

T = To + TGo + TPo + TGP (To find **TGo**)

TGo = T - To - TPo - TGP
TGo = 70 - 40 - TPo - 15
TGo = 15 - TPo

> [!info]  Equation 1
> TGo + TPo = 15

G = Go + TGo + GPo + TGP

TGo = G - Go - GPo - TGP
TGo = 65 - 20 - GPo - 15
TGo = 30 - GPo

> [!info] Equation 2
> TGo + GPo = 30

P = Po + TPo + GPo + TGP
85 = 45 + TPo + GPo + 15
85 = 60 + TPo + GPo

> [!info] Equation 3
> TPo + GPo = 25

> [!important] Add Equations 1, 2, and 3

TGo + TPo + TGo + GPo + TPo + GPo = 15 + 30 + 25
TGo + TGo + TPo + TPo + GPo + GPo = 70
2(TGo + TPo + GPo) = 70
TGo + TPo + GPo = 35

> [!important] Subtract Equation 3

TGo + TPo + GPo - TPo - GPo = 35 - 25

>[!info] TGo = 10

remember TG = TGo + TGP

TG = 10 + 15
TG = 25

> [!note] Answer
> The amount of people who applied for Daily Times and Graphic is 25.

## 2. Daily Times and Daily Punch

TP = TPo + TGP

from Question 1:

> [!info] Equation 1
> TGo + TPo + GPo = 35

>[!info] Equation 2
> TGo + GPo = 30

>[!important] Subtract Equation 2 from Equation 1

TGo + TPo + GPo - TGo - GPo = 35 - 30


>[!info] TPo = 5

remember TP = TPo + TGP
TP = 5 + 15
TP = 20

> [!note] Answer
> The amount of people who applied for Daily Times and Punch is 20.

## 3. Daily Graphics and Daily Punch

GP = GPo + TGP

from Question 1:

> [!info] Equation 1
> TGo + TPo + GPo = 35

> [!info] Equation 2
> TGo + TPo = 15

>[!important] Subtract Equation 2 from Equation 1

TGo + TPo + GPo - TGo - TPo = 35 - 15

>[!info] GPo = 20

remember GP = GPo + TGP
GP = 20 + 15
GP = 35

> [!note] Answer
> The amount of people who applied for Daily Graphic and Punch is 35.

## 4. At least one establishment

At least one = To + Go + Po + TGo + TPo + GPo + TGP

At least one = 40 + 20 + 45 + 10 + 5 + 20 +15
At least one = 155

> [!note] Answer
> The amount of people who applied to at least one establishment is 155.

## 5. Two establishments only

Two only = TGo + TPo + GPo

Two only = 10 + 5 + 20
Two only = 35

> [!note] Answer
> The amount of people who applied to two establishments only is 35.

## 6. Exactly one establishment

Exactly one = To + Go + Po

Exactly one = 40 + 20 + 45
Exactly one = 105

> [!note] Answer
> The amount of people who applied to exactly one establishment is 105.
