Give the grammatical name and grammatical function of the following underlined group of words:

1. <u>What was more painful</u> was that the girl was innocent.
	- **Grammatical Name**: Noun Clause
	- **Grammatical Function**: Subject of the verb *"was"*.
2. <u>The beautiful girl</u> with head bag should be the winner.
	- **Grammatical Name**: Noun Phrase
	- **Grammatical Function**: Subject of the sentence.
3. He reasoned <u>that leaving his two wives behind</u> was not the solution.
	- **Grammatical Name**: Noun Clause
	- **Grammatical Function**: Subject of the verb "was"
4. I wished <u>I could melt away before my father's eyes</u>.
	- **Grammatical Name**: Noun Clause
	- **Grammatical Function**: Object of the verb *"wished"*.
5. His wish that <u>I become his friend</u> is not possible.
	- **Grammatical Name**: Noun Clause
	- **Grammatical Function**: Subject of the verb *"is"*.
