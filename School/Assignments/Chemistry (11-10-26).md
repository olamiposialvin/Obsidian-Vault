# **Question 1**
**Explain why oxidation and reduction processes are complementary.**
Oxidation and reduction are complementary because they always occur together in a chemical reaction (known as a redox reaction). Oxidation is the loss of electrons, while reduction is the gain of electrons. For one species to lose electrons (oxidation), there must be another species present to accept those electrons (reduction). Therefore, the total number of electrons lost must equal the total number of electrons gained.

# **Question 2**
**Balance the following redox reaction:**

**Reaction 1:** $\text{MnO}_4^- + 2\text{I}^- \rightarrow \text{I}_2 + \text{Mn}^{2+}$
This reaction takes place in an acidic medium.
*   **Oxidation half-reaction:** $2\text{I}^- \rightarrow \text{I}_2 + 2e^-$
*   **Reduction half-reaction:** $\text{MnO}_4^- + 8\text{H}^+ + 5e^- \rightarrow \text{Mn}^{2+} + 4\text{H}_2\text{O}$
*   To balance electrons (LCM of 2 and 5 is 10), multiply the oxidation reaction by 5 and the reduction reaction by 2:
    *   $10\text{I}^- \rightarrow 5\text{I}_2 + 10e^-$
    *   $2\text{MnO}_4^- + 16\text{H}^+ + 10e^- \rightarrow 2\text{Mn}^{2+} + 8\text{H}_2\text{O}$
*   **Balanced Equation:**
    $$2\text{MnO}_4^- + 10\text{I}^- + 16\text{H}^+ \rightarrow 2\text{Mn}^{2+} + 5\text{I}_2 + 8\text{H}_2\text{O}$$

**Reaction 2:** $\text{Fe} + 2\text{Ag}^+ \rightarrow \text{Fe}^{2+} + 2\text{Ag}$
*   This equation is already balanced.
    *   Atoms: 1 Fe, 2 Ag on both sides.
    *   Charge: +2 on left ($2 \times +1$), +2 on right ($+2$).

**a. Write a balanced ionic half equation for the reaction**
*(Providing for both reactions listed)*

*   **For Reaction 1 ($\text{MnO}_4^- + \text{I}^-$):**
    *   Oxidation: $2\text{I}^- \rightarrow \text{I}_2 + 2e^-$
    *   Reduction: $\text{MnO}_4^- + 8\text{H}^+ + 5e^- \rightarrow \text{Mn}^{2+} + 4\text{H}_2\text{O}$

*   **For Reaction 2 ($\text{Fe} + \text{Ag}^+$):**
    *   Oxidation: $\text{Fe} \rightarrow \text{Fe}^{2+} + 2e^-$
    *   Reduction: $\text{Ag}^+ + e^- \rightarrow \text{Ag}$ (or $2\text{Ag}^+ + 2e^- \rightarrow 2\text{Ag}$)

**b. Which of the species is i. oxidized; reduced?**
*   **Reaction 1:**
    *   **Oxidized:** $\text{I}^-$ (Iodide ion) — oxidation number increases from -1 to 0.
    *   **Reduced:** $\text{MnO}_4^-$ (Permanganate ion, specifically Mn) — oxidation number decreases from +7 to +2.
*   **Reaction 2:**
    *   **Oxidized:** $\text{Fe}$ (Iron) — oxidation number increases from 0 to +2.
    *   **Reduced:** $\text{Ag}^+$ (Silver ion) — oxidation number decreases from +1 to 0.
