1. Define Simple Harmonic Motion. <br>
	**Simple Harmonic Motion (SHM)** is the periodic motion of a body or particle along a straight line such that its acceleration is directly proportional to its displacement from a fixed point and is always directed towards that point. <br>
	The particle moves to and fro under the influence of a restoring force, which directs it back to its equilibrium position. <br><br>
2. An object of mass $0.6 \text{kg}$ oscillates with a period of $2$ seconds and an amplitude of $4 \text{cm}$. Calculate its maximum kinetic energy. <br>

	m = 0.6 \text{kg}
	\\
	T = 2 \text{s}
	\\

	$$

	$$

	{2}
	$$
	$$

	{\cancelto{2}{4} \pi^2 m A^2}
	{\cancel{2}T^2}
	$$
	$$

	{2 \pi^2 m A^2}
	{T^2}
	$$
	$$
