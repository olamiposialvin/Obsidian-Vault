# Question 1

State the meaning of capitalist democracy.

Capitalist democracy is a system which the private sector produce goods and services for free market and the government is elected to maintain and promote orderliness in the society.

It is a system that allows individuals to handle the production, exchange and distribution of goods and services with a guideline from the government.

# Question 3

Itemize four (4) advantages and disadvantages of capitalist democracy.

## Advantages

1. It enhances faster economic development.
2. It guarantees maximum freedom of enterprises.
3. It encourages specialization in skills and quality productivity.
4. It allows room for invention, innovation and creativity.

## Disadvantages

1. It encourages inequitable distribution of income and wealth.
2. It leads to unemployment and cheap labour.
3. It encourages unhealthy competition and rivalry.
4. It promotes exploitation of the poor and less privileged in the society.

# Question 5

Discuss how political parties serve as a link between governments and citizens.

Political parties carry the views of the people to the government and the plans of the government to the people.

1. They collect and present the interests of different groups to the government.
2. They educate citizens through campaigns, rallies and pamphlets.
3. They identify the problems facing the state and propose solutions.
4. They recruit leaders through elections, so citizens choose who governs.

# Question 6

How do political parties use manifestos as a tool to attract voter support in elections?

1. It tells voters what the party will do in government.
2. It is sold to the electorates during campaigns and rallies.
3. It shows how the party differs from others, so voters can compare.
4. It builds trust, because voters can hold the party to its promises after the election.
